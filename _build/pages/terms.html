Store policies

The five rules we stand by.

Every purchase at Monalisa Nxt Mall is covered by these straightforward terms — know them before you shop. Fair policies are how we've earned Godda's trust since 1999.

01 Exchange only

Exchange, not refund

Only exchange is available at Monalisa Nxt Mall. No money will be refunded for an exchange — the exchange value is adjusted against your next purchase from the store. Cash refunds are not applicable under any circumstances.

02 Jurisdiction

Godda jurisdiction

These terms and conditions are applied and shall be followed under the jurisdiction of Godda, Jharkhand. Any matter arising from a purchase shall be subject to the courts of Godda only.

03 Offer period items

Offer purchases — no exchange, no guarantee

If any product is purchased under an offer period, it will not be exchangeable. Offer items also do not come under any guarantee for colour fading or any such issue.

04 Genuine defects

Defects — 7 to 10 days

We do not give a guarantee on any product, as clothing may carry manufacturing defects. However, if the defect is genuine and the customer comes within 1 week (maximum 10 days) of purchase, we may consider changing or replacing the product.

05 Hygiene first

Undergarments — non-exchangeable

For your health and hygiene, undergarments are non-exchangeable. Please check the size and fit carefully before purchase.

06 No warranty

Ride-on toy cars — no warranty

Ride-on toy cars do not come under any warranty — neither from the manufacturer nor from Monalisa Nxt Mall. Please inspect the car carefully at the time of purchase; once taken home, no exchange or replacement applies.
