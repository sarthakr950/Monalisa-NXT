Questions, answered

No — we don't run everyday discounts. Whenever one of our brands runs an offer (festive, seasonal or anniversary), the same offer is live at our store and applied automatically at billing. Follow @monalisa_nxtmall_godda on Instagram to know the moment an offer starts.

We offer exchange only — no cash refunds. Unused items with tags and the original bill can be exchanged within 7 days of purchase, and the exchange amount is adjusted against your next purchase in the store. Terms apply in the Godda jurisdiction, and offer-period purchases, undergarments and items with genuine wear are not exchangeable. See our Terms & Conditions page for full details.
